HELP

Common Playback Problems

Video playback can vary depending on the file, codecs, resolution and Android device.

Video Does Not Play

Check whether the file is complete and whether its audio and video codecs are compatible with the application and Android device. Try another known-compatible video file to compare playback.

No Audio

Check the device volume and make sure the video actually contains an audio track. Some files use audio codecs that may not be available on every Android device.

Subtitle Not Showing

Make sure the subtitle file is compatible and correctly selected. When using an external subtitle file, check that the file is readable and matches the video.

Playback Is Not Smooth

High-resolution or high-bitrate videos can require more processing power. Try a lower-resolution copy or check the device's available storage and background activity.

TECHNICAL NOTES

Understanding Video Compatibility

Why two files with similar extensions can behave differently during playback.

Container vs Codec

A video extension such as MP4 or MKV describes the container. The actual video and audio streams inside the container use codecs. Both the container and codecs need to be compatible for reliable playback.

Video Codec

Video streams can use different compression technologies. Device hardware and Android media components determine which video codecs can be decoded efficiently.

Audio Codec

A video can display correctly while its audio fails if the audio stream uses a codec that is unavailable on the device or unsupported by the playback stack.

Device Hardware

Resolution, bitrate and codec complexity can affect playback performance. Older or less powerful devices may require more processing for high-quality video.
